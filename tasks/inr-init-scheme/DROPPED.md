# Dropped

The measured candidates change both the initialization rule and the SIREN frequency
parameters. That makes the current evidence non-identifiable and duplicates the dedicated
frequency surface in `inr-per-layer-w0`.

The research question itself is salvageable. Re-enable it only after exposing a narrow
initialization-policy configuration at one fixed SIREN frequency and measuring every
candidate on all three settings. The existing measurements cannot be reused for that
redesign.
